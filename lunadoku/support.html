Lunadoku Support

Help with puzzles, the Nightly, cards and purchases.

Contact

Open a support request. Include your iPhone model and iOS version if the problem is technical.

Common questions

How do I play?
Fill the grid so every row, every column and every 3×3 box holds the numbers 1 to 9 once each. Drag a tile from the tray and set it down in a square. If you prefer tapping, open the menu, tap Settings and set Placing tiles to Tap. To see the introduction again, open the menu (the round button in the top-right corner) and tap How to play.

What do Notes, Erase, Undo and Guide do?
Notes lets you pencil small candidate numbers into a square. Erase clears the square you have chosen. Undo takes back your last move. Guide gives a gentle hint when you are stuck.

What is the Nightly?
One puzzle a night, the same for everyone. A new Nightly arrives at 5 am local time, so a late-night puzzle still counts for the night you started it. Each Nightly is played in the theme the moon chooses that night, even if you don't own it yet. If you would rather always play in your own theme, turn on Always use my theme in Settings. Solve it to earn a card.

What is Classic?
A new puzzle whenever you wish, at the difficulty you choose, in your own theme.

How do the cards work?
Each Nightly you solve earns a card for that night's theme and moon phase, in a Satin, Foil or Holographic finish. A full-moon night earns a special edition. Find them in the menu under Cards.

I bought the Core Pack and it is missing.
Open the menu, tap Settings, and choose Restore Purchases. Purchases belong to your Apple Account, so make sure you are signed in to the App Store with the same one.

My cards did not move to my new iPhone.
Your games and cards are saved on the iPhone itself, not on a server. They move with an iPhone backup or a device-to-device transfer. A purchase comes back on any device with Restore Purchases.

Can I remove the ads?
Yes. The Core Pack removes ads for good and adds every theme. Ads never appear while you are solving.

The board doesn't light up when I finish a row.
Check Motion in Settings. Follow iPhone uses your iPhone's Reduce Motion setting; choose Full for the full effects or Reduced for a calmer version.

No sound or music?
Check the ring/silent switch and the Sound and Music settings in the app. Music stays quiet while other audio is playing.

Privacy

No accounts; anonymous statistics you can turn off; non-personalised ads unless you own the Core Pack. Read the privacy policy.
