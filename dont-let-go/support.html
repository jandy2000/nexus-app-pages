Don't Let Go Support

Help with the board, the journal and purchases.

Contact

Open a support request. Include your iPhone model and iOS version if the problem is technical, and what the spirit was doing if it is about a séance.

Common questions

How do I play?
Hold one finger on the planchette and keep it there. The spirit moves the planchette and your finger goes with it. To ask a question, slide the planchette onto one of the paper notes beside the board and rest it there until the line under the question fills. To answer the spirit, slide to YES or NO. To end the séance properly, rest on the painted GOOD BYE when it glows.

The séance ended and it says FOLLOWED.
Your finger left the planchette before the spirit said goodbye. A very short slip is forgiven, but a real lift lets go, and the spirit follows you. That is the game's one rule. It will be waiting next time.

I fell behind the planchette.
That never ends a séance. The spirit waits for your hand; put your finger back on the planchette and it carries on.

Do I need headphones?
No, but they are strongly recommended. Many sounds move around and behind you, and that only works in headphones. With AirPods, head tracking keeps each sound in its place when you turn your head.

Where is my journal?
On the opening card, tap Journal. Each spirit you have met has a page; swipe between them. Pages show only spirits you have met.

How do I start over?
When the board's story has ended, the last page of the journal offers Begin again (a new board). Otherwise, deleting and reinstalling the app starts a new board.

"The candle is spent." What now?
Your séances for tonight are used up. The candle burns again after six in the evening, local time. If your version offers the unlimited-séances unlock, it is on the same card.

I bought unlimited séances and it is missing.
When the candle is spent, tap Restore purchase on that card. Purchases belong to your Apple Account, so make sure you are signed in to the App Store with the same one.

My journal did not move to my new iPhone.
The journal is saved on the iPhone itself, not on a server. It moves with an iPhone backup or a device-to-device transfer. A purchase comes back on any device with Restore.

Is any of this real?
No. Don't Let Go is a work of fiction. No real spirits are contacted, and the app makes no claims about the supernatural. If the dark gets to be too much, take your finger off the planchette, turn the lights on, and come back another night.
