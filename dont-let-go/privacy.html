Privacy Policy

Don't Let Go: Séance for iPhone. Last updated October 17, 2026.

The short version: Don't Let Go does not collect personal information. There are no accounts, no advertising and no tracking. The app sends anonymous usage statistics so we can improve the game, and you can turn them off. Your journal and anything you spell at the board stay on your iPhone.

What stays on your phone

  • Your journal. The spirits you have met, the clues you have found, how each séance ended, and anything you spell for a spirit during play (for example, letters you give it) are stored on your iPhone in the app's own storage. They are not sent to us. Deleting the app deletes them, and "Begin again (a new board)" in the journal clears them. An iPhone backup includes them.
  • Tonight's séances. The app counts the séances you have held tonight on your iPhone, so it knows when the candle is spent.

What the app uses while you play, and never keeps

  • Head tracking. With AirPods or other supported headphones, the app reads your head's movement so each sound stays in its place in the room when you turn. iOS asks your permission first. The movement is used live and is never stored or sent anywhere.
  • The time. Some spirits know what time it is. The app reads your iPhone's clock for that, and nothing else about where you are.
  • No camera, no microphone, no contacts, no photos and no location. The app never asks for them.

Purchases

If your version of the app offers the unlimited-séances unlock, the purchase is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, your Apple Account or your payment details. The app checks the purchase with Apple directly on your iPhone; there is no server of ours in between.

Anonymous usage statistics

Don't Let Go sends anonymous usage statistics (for example, that a séance ended at peace after three questions) to TelemetryDeck so we can improve the game. They carry a random identifier created on your device, never your name, contact details, advertising identifier or location, and they are not used for tracking or advertising. Nothing you type or spell is sent. Each statistic also carries the app version, the iOS version, the device type (iPhone), your region setting and the day (not the time).

You can turn this off in the iPhone Settings app: Settings > Don't Let Go > Privacy > Share Anonymous Statistics. When you turn it off, the app sends one last note that statistics were turned off, deletes the random identifier, and sends nothing more.

Ads and tracking

The app contains no advertising and no tracking of any kind, and it never asks to track you. The only third-party code is TelemetryDeck's, used for the statistics above. If you have chosen in your iPhone's settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may pass us anonymous crash reports and usage statistics; that is Apple's feature and you can turn it off at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements.

Children

Don't Let Go is a horror game rated 13+ and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. The anonymous statistics cannot be connected to you, so there is nothing for us to look up, correct, or delete; turning them off deletes the random identifier on your iPhone.
