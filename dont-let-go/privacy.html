Privacy Policy

Don't Let Go: Séance for iPhone. Last updated October 20, 2026.

The short version: Don't Let Go does not collect personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no servers of ours. What happens at the board stays on your iPhone.

What stays on your phone

  • Your journal. The spirits you have met, the clues you have found, how each séance ended, and anything you spell for a spirit during play (for example, letters you give it) are stored on your iPhone in the app's own storage. They are not sent to us. Deleting the app deletes them, and "Begin again (a new board)" in the journal clears them. An iPhone backup includes them.
  • Tonight's séances. The app counts the séances you have held tonight on your iPhone, so it knows when the candle is spent.

What the app uses while you play, and never keeps

  • Head tracking. With AirPods or other supported headphones, the app reads your head's movement so each sound stays in its place in the room when you turn. iOS asks your permission first. The movement is used live and is never stored or sent anywhere.
  • The time. Some spirits know what time it is. The app reads your iPhone's clock for that, and nothing else about where you are.
  • No camera, no microphone, no contacts, no photos and no location. The app never asks for them.

Purchases

If your version of the app offers the unlimited-séances unlock, the purchase is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, your Apple Account or your payment details. The app checks the purchase with Apple directly on your iPhone; there is no server of ours in between.

Analytics, ads and tracking

The app contains no analytics, no advertising, no third-party code and no tracking of any kind. If you have chosen in your iPhone's settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may pass us anonymous crash reports and usage statistics; that is Apple's feature and you can turn it off at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements.

Children

Don't Let Go is a horror game rated 13+ and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Because the app collects nothing, there is nothing for us to request, correct, or delete.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date before that version is released.
