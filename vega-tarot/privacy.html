Privacy Policy

Vega Tarot for iPhone. Last updated September 18, 2026.

The short version: Vega Tarot does not collect, store, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no servers of ours.

What stays on your phone

  • Your questions and readings. Every reading is generated on your device. The questions you type are never sent anywhere.
  • Your journal. Saved readings and notes are stored only on your device, in the app's own storage. Deleting the app deletes them.
  • Your settings. Preferences such as reversals, card motion, and sound are stored on your device.

Purchases

The one-time purchase is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. Apple's handling of purchases is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

On-device intelligence

On devices that support Apple Intelligence, the app may use Apple's on-device language model to rephrase Vega's lines. This runs entirely on your phone. Nothing is transmitted.

Crisis resources

If a question suggests you may be in crisis, the app pauses and shows crisis resources such as the 988 Lifeline. Tapping a resource opens your phone's dialer, messages, or browser. The app records nothing about it.

Children

Vega Tarot does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Because the app collects nothing, there is nothing to request, correct, or delete.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.

Contact
