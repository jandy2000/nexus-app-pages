Common questions

I bought and the spreads are still locked.
Open Settings in the app and tap Restore a previous purchase. Purchases are tied to your Apple ID, so make sure you are signed in with the same one.

Where are my saved readings?
In the Journal, on your device only. They are not backed up to any server. An iPhone backup includes them.

The cards do not move.
Card motion is a switch in the app's Settings. If it is on and the cards still do not move, restart the app.

Can Vega predict my future?
No. Vega Tarot is for entertainment and reflection. The cards suggest; they do not decide. For medical, legal, financial, or mental-health questions, talk to a qualified person.

I am in crisis.
If you are in the United States, call or text 988. Anywhere else, contact your local emergency number.
