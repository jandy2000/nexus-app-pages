Privacy Policy

Sniper Wire for iPhone. Last updated October 5, 2026.

The short version: Sniper Wire does not collect personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no servers of ours. Your progress stays on your iPhone. The optional services the game uses, Game Center and in-app purchases, are run by Apple.

What stays on your phone

  • Your progress. Operations completed, stars, difficulty, range cards, in-game cash, your rifles, parts and loadout, best scores, your daily streak, your place on the club ladder and your settings (shooting mode, aim speed, left-handed layout, haptics and volume) are stored on your iPhone in the app's own storage. They are not sent to us. Deleting the app deletes them. An iPhone backup includes them.
  • Ranked scores waiting to be sent. If you post a Daily Shot or Weekly Course score while Game Center is off or you are offline, the score is kept on your iPhone and sent to Game Center the next time you sign in, as long as its day or week is still running.
  • A Field Kit marker. So the Field Kit's in-game cash is paid out only once, the app stores Apple's transaction number for that purchase in your iPhone's keychain. It stays on this device only, is not synced to iCloud, and remains if you delete the app, so a reinstall does not pay it twice. It contains no name, email or payment detail.

Game Center

Game Center is optional. If you are signed in, the app sends your ranked Daily Shot and Weekly Course scores to Apple's Game Center leaderboards, and, if you start a head-to-head match, it sends the match's course and your shots, labelled with your Game Center player ID, to Apple so your opponent's phone can play them back. Other players see your Game Center name and scores, as with any Game Center game. While a head-to-head match is in progress, the app keeps the match's ID and your opponent's Game Center name on your iPhone so it can show who you are playing; nothing about Game Center is sent anywhere other than Apple. Game Center is covered by Apple's privacy policy, and you can sign out in your iPhone's Settings.

Purchases

Purchases (rifles, the Field Kit and cash packs) are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, your Apple Account or your payment details. The app checks what you own with Apple directly on your iPhone; there is no server of ours in between.

Sharing a score

When you share a Daily Shot result, the app opens the iPhone share sheet with a line of text. Where it goes is your choice; the app does not send it anywhere itself.

Analytics, ads and tracking

The app contains no analytics, no advertising, no third-party code and no tracking of any kind. If you have chosen in your iPhone's settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may pass us anonymous crash reports and usage statistics; that is Apple's feature and you can turn it off at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements.

Children

Sniper Wire is rated 18+ and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Because the app collects nothing, there is nothing for us to request, correct, or delete.

Changes

If this policy changes, for example if a future version adds anonymous usage statistics, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date before that version is released.
