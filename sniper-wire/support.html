Sniper Wire Support

Help with the game, purchases, and Game Center.

Contact

Open a support request. Include your iPhone model and iOS version if the problem is technical, and the operation's name if it is about a mission.

Common questions

How do I get to Velarra?
Velarra is free. Its four operations open in order once Karsk's six are complete and you have enough campaign stars (12 for the first). Replaying a Karsk operation for a cleaner result (a higher score, no extra rounds) can earn more stars.

A rifle or the Field Kit I bought is missing.
Open Settings (the gear on the home screen) and tap Restore purchases, or in the Armory tap the BANKED line and then Restore. Purchases belong to your Apple Account, so make sure you are signed in to the App Store with the same one. The Field Kit's cash is paid once per device: if this iPhone has had it before, Restore will not pay it again.

I bought a cash pack and it did not come back after a reinstall.
Cash packs are used up when they are banked, so the App Store does not restore them. Your rifles and the bundle always come back with Restore.

My progress did not move to my new iPhone.
Progress is saved on the iPhone itself, not on a server. It moves with an iPhone backup or a device-to-device transfer. Purchases come back on any device with Restore.

My ranked score is not on the leaderboard.
Ranked scores need Game Center. If you were offline or signed out, the game holds the score and sends it the next time you sign in, as long as that day's Daily Shot or that week's Weekly Course is still running. Check that Game Center is on in your iPhone's Settings.

An operation is too hard.
Try Recruit difficulty, or switch to ARCADE in Settings (the gear on the home screen), which marks where the round will land. and read the briefing's data card: it gives the hold for the range. Wind matters more than anything at distance. Where OPAL calls it, dial or hold for the call; where there is no call, read the steam, the dust or the shimmer. Moving targets need lead.

Do I have to pay to finish the game?
No. All 10 operations, in Karsk and Velarra, are free and balanced on the issued rifle. Rifles, parts and cash can all be earned in play; buying them is only a shortcut.

Is there a subscription?
No. Every purchase is one-time, apart from the optional cash packs, which you buy only when you want them.
